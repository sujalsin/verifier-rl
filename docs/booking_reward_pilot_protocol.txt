ISOLATION, FAILURE AND RESOURCE ENVELOPE
Reuse the established Modal CPU image/runner: fresh sandbox per source/input,
network/secrets/mounts/OIDC disabled, privilege drop, CPU/memory/output/wall limits,
expected answers and scoring outside candidate control. Never run candidate code
on laptop, trusted controller or training worker. Eight concurrent workers,
.26-second creation pacing. Per-attempt intent and raw result are durably journaled
before derived scoring. A partial batch never grants permission to replay it.

At most one startup replacement per source/input, sixteen globally, only for
previously reviewed confirmed pre-candidate failures with cleanup: preflight RPC
command-start timeout or trusted preflight SIGKILL with no candidate submission.
Keep both attempts; unknown cleanup, candidate signals/timeouts and ordinary bad
answers are not retryable. Other unresolved results stop scoring, never become zero.
Whole-sandbox cleanup completes for in-flight workers after another worker fails.
Nonpreemptible CPU controller/grading functions, provider retries=0, atomic claims
prevent controller/training replay. GPU interruption stops; no automatic retraining.

Max starts: 384 controls + 18,432 training + 13,776 evaluation + 16 startup
replacements = 32,608. Rejected extraction can reduce actual execution count.
Two GPU calls at most, 7,200 seconds each; one CPU controller at most, 21,600
seconds; each of 61 grading calls at most 1,800 seconds; shared absolute deadline.
Request/limit: GPU 2 CPU/32 GiB, CPU functions 1 CPU/2 GiB, candidate sandbox
1 CPU/.25 GiB with 120-second maximum lifetime. Preserve existing per-process
128-MiB, 2-CPU-second, 5-wall-second limits and 45-second transport limit.
Fresh rates/account billing and prior reservations are saved before launch.
The all-resources-at-timeout reservation is conservative, not an invoice or
provider hard spending cap. Do not expand after failures or inconclusive results.
