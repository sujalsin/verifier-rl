Budget reconciliation, 2026-10-01
=================================

Problem
-------
The application's guard balance was described as spending. It is not a
provider bill. It is the sum of resource-duration estimates for settled calls,
maximum reservations for unresolved calls, and a $20 overhead allowance.
Interrupted calls retained their full maximum reservations even after the
provider had terminal timing records. This could stop admission prematurely.

The Modal dashboard covers a different scope and billing period, may include
storage, and may show credit-adjusted amounts. It must not replace this study's
cumulative ledger. The compute ceiling remains $250; storage is separate.

---------------------

The proposed reduction in their unused maximum holds was $74.1279266.
Only these worker reservations qualify: candidate sandboxes were reserved

Live-writer safety
------------------
Do not directly edit the Dict ledger while its serialized owner is running.
Its whole-ledger get/put updates are not compare-and-swap transactions.

An isolated fake-writer autoscaler test proved that setting max_containers=0
does not reliably pause new input. That method was rejected; it made no live
research-service changes.

The user authorized a graceful accounting handoff. A clearly labelled
temporary admission escrow is reserved through the EXISTING sole writer.
It represents no compute allocation and is not actual spending. It fills
headroom so the existing guard declines new batches; bounded_map then lets
every already-admitted batch finish before propagating BudgetReached. No
FunctionCall is cancelled and no live container is terminated.

The new writer refuses to take ownership until the old app has stopped.
The handoff requires all claimed batches to have completed receipts, no
unknown outcomes, and no old execution intent for any remaining program.
It verifies retained raw evidence and partitions all 480 fixed batches.

Before publishing a corrected ledger, the successor commits the before/after
transaction and provider evidence to both work and archive Volumes. Recovery
accepts only the exact before or after state and cannot release a hold twice.
The admission-only escrow is then settled to zero. The $250 ceiling, $20
allowance, other holds, and all research artifacts are preserved.

The successor retains four workers, the existing start gate, frozen candidate
runner/scoring/tests, saved sample identities, and the existing evaluation
deadline. It adds a cooperative drain hook for future maintenance. Training,
GRPO, reward functions, and checkpoints are unchanged.
